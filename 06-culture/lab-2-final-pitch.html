M1 · Strategy canvas

We promise verified market intelligence, so trust comes before speed.

Hallucination-free traceability

Every factual claim is backed by a citation that supports the stated value. Target: drive the defect rate toward zero.

Latency

p95 time to a completed response, tracked separately for look-ups and multi-competitor comparisons.

Robustness

On out-of-scope or ambiguous questions, decline or flag the data gap rather than fabricate.

Trade-off we chose: low hallucination over latency. A wrong value shown to a client's leadership can cost a $50k+ contract; a slower answer only costs convenience. Target user: VP-level strategists and product leaders at Fortune 500 companies.

M4 · Eval gates and launch strategy

The Hard gate is zero stale or contradicted claims rendered.

SeverityMetricThreshold
HARDStale, unsupported or contradicted claims rendered= 0%; false-block ≤ 5%

ADVISORYFlagged slang≤ 5%

Per-dimension CI gate

Blocking: faithfulness (floor 95), task completion (90), safety (98). Warn-only: tool selection, latency, cost. Replayed on a frozen golden set of at least 30 cases.

Staged rollout

5% → 25% → 100%, advancing only while Soft gates hold.

A staged rollout limits who sees a miss. It does not stop a screenshot of one wrong price from spreading, which is why the Hard gate has to pass first.
